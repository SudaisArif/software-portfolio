INSIDE THE INSTRUMENT

The sound is
the substance.

Every note starts as a tiny burst of noise. It travels around a tuned delay loop, losing energy and high frequencies on each pass. That is a physical model of a plucked string.

Pluck→String loop→Moving delay→Stereo + room

01 / The string

Fractional tuning, frequency-dependent loss, excitation-position notches, and independent overlapping voices. Power changes excitation and brightness; loft adds a smooth brightness contour.

engine/dsp.mjs → StringVoice

02 / The flight

Propagation time changes as the sounding ball moves past a fixed listener. A fractional delay reads the waveform at a changing speed, producing Doppler pitch motion. Stable pitch bypasses the changing delay while retaining tone, distance and stereo expression.

engine/dsp.mjs → Engine.tick

03 / The space

Distance attenuation, a smoothed air-loss filter, equal-power stereo panning, and six damped feedback delays create the listening environment.

engine/dsp.mjs → Stadium

04 / Your composition

Keep a shot to save its notes, tempo, key guide, trajectory, synthesis settings, seed and live sound changes. WAV export re-renders that performance. JSON keeps the instructions; MIDI exports the underlying score.
